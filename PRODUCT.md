# Product

## Platform
web

## Primary user
Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating David Machado for backend and full-stack
internships or junior roles. Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, and direct links,
often on mobile, and need a fast, credible read of his engineering work.

## Product purpose
Make professional evidence inspectable: three projects with public repositories or a
stated private case study, the architecture decisions behind them, and the test surface
that backs the claims. Convert interest into an email conversation.

## Differentiation
An entry-level profile with production-shaped artifacts: a .NET 10 marketplace core with
592 automated tests and a TypeScript lease billing engine, both open source. The site is
trilingual (en/es/pt) and shows evidence instead of marketing claims.

## Durable constraints
- Dark-first visual system with accessible contrast.
- Preserve internationalization (en/es/pt) and keep every translation key in sync.
- Preserve SEO metadata, structured data, and social preview assets.
- Preserve accessibility: keyboard navigation, reduced motion, semantic landmarks,
  skip link, and labeled controls.
- Keep every content and stack claim aligned with a repository or a verifiable source.

## Stack
React 18, TypeScript, Vite 6, Tailwind CSS 4, Motion. Deployed as a static site on
Vercel at https://davidmachado-dev.vercel.app/.
