# Product

## Platform
web

## Primary user
Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating David Machado's technical profile are the priority audience. Freelance clients and prospective employers are secondary. Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, and direct links, often on mobile, and need a fast, credible read of backend and architecture experience.

## Product purpose
A personal portfolio that makes David Machado's software engineering experience concrete and easy to evaluate, and converts interest into contact or interview conversations. It should surface real project work, stack, and engineering approach rather than generic marketing copy.

## Differentiation
Honest semi-senior positioning backed by verified experience, multilingual content (es/en/pt), and an engineering-focused presentation (architecture, databases, delivery practices) instead of a purely visual showcase.

## Durable constraints
- Theme must be dynamic and variable, not locked to a single dark presentation; light/dark and accent variation should remain possible.
- Preserve internationalization (es/en/pt).
- Preserve SEO metadata, structured data, and social preview assets.
- Preserve accessibility (keyboard navigation, reduced motion, semantic landmarks, skip link).
- Preserve performance characteristics (lazy/efficient motion, optimized assets).
- Keep content and stack claims aligned with verified experience.

## Stack
React 18, TypeScript, Vite 6, Tailwind CSS 4, Motion, React Router 7. Deployed as a static site at https://davidmachado.dev/.
